# Getting Started
New workspaces include a 14-day free trial of the Pro plan with no credit card required.
You can invite team members from Settings > Team; Starter supports up to 3 members, Pro up to 20 and Business is unlimited.
Integrations with Slack, Google Sheets and HubSpot can be connected from the Integrations page.
A guided setup checklist appears on the dashboard for the first 7 days.
