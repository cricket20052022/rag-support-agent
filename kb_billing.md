# Billing and Plans
Northwind Cloud offers three plans: Starter at $29 per month, Pro at $99 per month and Business at $299 per month.
Annual billing gives a 20% discount on any plan.
Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month and can be downloaded from Settings > Billing.
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and bank transfer for annual Business plans.
If a payment fails, we retry the card three times over seven days before pausing the account.
