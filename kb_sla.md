# Uptime and Support SLA
The Business plan includes a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA.
If uptime falls below the SLA, customers receive service credits of 10% of the monthly fee per 0.1% below target.
Support hours are Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm Singapore time, for Starter and Pro.
Business customers get 24/7 support with a 1-hour first response time for critical issues.
The current system status is published at status.northwind.example.
